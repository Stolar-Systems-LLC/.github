Stolar Systems helps teams strategize, implement, and build AI systems tailored to their workflows — with the flexibility and prioritization that larger firms cannot offer.

## AI consulting with an engineering mentality

Founded by a mechanical engineer turned AI researcher, the firm pairs industry engineering consulting experience with a graduate background in machine learning research. That combination shows up in how we scope projects, how we validate systems against the constraints they actually operate under, and how we communicate with the engineering teams already doing the work.

## Four engagements, made to fit

Most clients start with one of these. Where you begin depends on where you are, and we'll help you figure that out.

**01 / Strategize** — Determine where AI fits your business and where it doesn't.

**02 / Implement** — Integrate your vetted AI pipelines and learn how to leverage them.

**03 / Build** — Customize production-ready AI models when off-the-shelf falls short.

**04 / Sustain** — Ongoing advisory and stewardship for deployed systems.
